A single flap carrying a status word: `ok` (flap-ink on flap), `warn` (on-yellow on solari-yellow), `alert` (flap-ink on flap-alert red) and `off` (board-muted).

Always give it a word — colour alone never carries the status. Use it for LIVE beside a board, a remark outside a board, or a one-line notice (PLATFORM 9 · 2 MIN). It does not animate.
